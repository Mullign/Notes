### Module Learning Objectives:

**Learners will be able to...**

1. describe the scope and goals of generative AI **(CLO 1)**.
2. explain the fundamentals of natural language processing (NLP), including key tasks such as tokenization and language modeling **(CLO 1, CLO 2)**.
3. represent words as vectors and explain how these representations capture semantic similarity **(CLO 1, CLO 2)**.
4. compare traditional statistical language models (e.g., n-gram models) with modern neural approaches, identifying their strengths, limitations, and use cases **(CLO 1, CLO 2)**.
