## Course-Level Objectives:

- **CO 1:** Explain the fundamentals of a wide range of algorithm design and analysis technique.
    
- **CO 2:** Identify and replicate standard algorithms for standard problems.
    
- **CO 3:** Implement algorithmic ideas in code.
