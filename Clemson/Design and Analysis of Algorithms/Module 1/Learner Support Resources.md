## Program Support

If you have questions regarding technical issues with the Coursera platform, course registration, tuition or fees, or other degree-related issues, please reach out to [**mscscoursera@clemson.edu**](mailto:mscscoursera@clemson.edu "Email Program Support")_._ For correspondence related to the content of this course, direct messaging to Dr. Dean or Korey on Slack is the preferred method; emailing them is also fine (note that this method of communication is specific to this course -- for other courses in our MS degree program, you may be asked to use mscscoursera@clemson.edu for all correspondence).

Note that the mccscoursera@clemson.edu email is monitored by Coursera and Clemson staff to route your inquiry to the appropriate resource. Therefore, any information transmitted to or from this email address may be accessed and reviewed by other authorized personnel. If you have an issue you prefer to discuss privately, we recommend providing only general information, so your issue can be routed to the appropriate resource for further discussion.

## Slack Channel

Slack is a tool for you to get to know your peers and build community within the Master of Science in Computer Science program. Each degree course has a public channel in Slack that you can join to chat in real-time with all your classmates.

Please log in directly to Master of Science in Computer Science Slack workspace at [https://join.slack.com/t/master-cs-clemson/signup](https://join.slack.com/t/master-cs-clemson/signup "Sign Up for a New Account with the Master of Science in Computer Science Slack") using your **@clemson.edu** email address. Course staff will be reachable on Slack, as this is our preferred method of communicating. Questions about the class can be directed to Dr. Dean or Korey (your TA) as private messages or in the class channel. If you are unable to see the #cpsc8400-fall-2025 channel, please send a message to Korey or reach out in the #general channel on Slack asking for an invite.
