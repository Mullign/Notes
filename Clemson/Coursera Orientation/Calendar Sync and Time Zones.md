## Mobile Calendar Sync 

Never miss a deadline by adding your deadlines to your calendar. You can sync your calendar deadlines on your desktop and on your mobile device. 

 [Learn more about Calendar Sync.](https://learner.coursera.help/hc/en-us/articles/360030934191-Add-Coursera-Deadlines-To-Your-Calendar "Add course deadlines to your calendar Article") 

##  Time Zones 

Assignment deadlines and live events are displayed based on the time zone you have set in your [Account Settings](https://www.coursera.org/account-settings "Your Account Settings page"). Some students prefer to set their time zones to their local time, while others like to set it based on that of the institution. You can manually update your time zone preference in your Coursera profile.

If your user account time zone setting doesn’t match your machine, you’ll receive a notification suggesting to change it in your account setting.
