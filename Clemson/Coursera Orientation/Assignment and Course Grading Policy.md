Instructors may use plus/minus grading systems for graduate students in graduate-level (6000+) courses at their sole discretion. There is no expectation or mandate that instructors should use plus/minus grades. For those faculty using this option, the following grades will be made available: A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C, C-, F. Under no circumstance will an A+ be awarded.

Instructors will describe their grading policy for a course, including whether they will use +/- grading, in the course syllabus and Course Introduction / Overview for each course in this program. Where policies refer to “a B average,” Clemson interprets that as meaning a 3.0 GPA. Quality points will be assigned as follows:

- A: 4.00
    
- A-: 3.66
    
- B+: 3.34
    
- B: 3.00
    
- B-: 2.66
    
- C+: 2.34
    
- C: 2.00
    
- C-: 1.66
    
- F: 0.00
    

Learners should be aware that if they fall below a cumulative 3.0 GPA, they will be placed on academic probation. You can reference the [Handbook](https://media.clemson.edu/graduate/website/files/pdfs/policyhandbook-spring2025.pdf "Clemson Graduate School Student Handbook") for more details and corrective actions if this should happen to you.
