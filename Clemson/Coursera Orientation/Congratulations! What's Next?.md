If you choose to take the Pathway (PBA) courses for entry into the MSCS Program, you will need to receive a B (3.0 GPA) or higher in the two PBA courses, **CPSC 8400 Design and Analysis of Algorithms** and **CPSC 8740 AI-Receptive Software Development**. After completion of the two Pathway courses with a B (3.0 GPA) or higher, learners will need to submit an official transcript.
