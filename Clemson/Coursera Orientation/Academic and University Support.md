There are two ways to receive program level support:

1. Post in the Coursera discussion forums
    
2. Send a message to [**mscscoursera@clemson.edu**](mailto:mscscoursera@clemson.edu "Email Program Support")
    

## **Discussion Forums**

Our program staff are here to support your learning. If you have any questions about the content covered in a course, please post it in the discussion forum. We will get back to you within 24 hours. By posting in the forum, you are also letting other students benefit from the response. More information about where to find and how to use discussion forums will be covered in Module 6.

## **Program Support Email Address:** [**mscscoursera@clemson.edu**](mailto:mscscoursera@clemson.edu "Email Program Support")*****

- Your grades or assignments**

If you have an issue you prefer to discuss privately, we recommend providing only general information, so your issue can be routed to the appropriate resource for further discussion.
