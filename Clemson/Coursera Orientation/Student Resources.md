## Student Accessibility Services

Clemson provides a range of accommodations for students to ensure that every Tiger has equal access to academic resources, campus life, and more. The Office of Student Accessibility Services routinely collaborates with faculty, staff, and departments through universal design principles and creative solutions to facilitate student access. Learn more about available services by visiting the [Student Accessibility Services website](https://www.clemson.edu/academics/student-accessibility-services/index.html "Student Accessibility Services website"). You can also access Clemson's Accessibility Web Portal by [clicking here](https://www.clemson.edu/accessibility/index.html "Clemson's Accessibility Web Portal"), where you will find more information and be able report any accessibility barriers you may encounter.

## Center for Career and Professional Development

The career center offers many unique services, such as career workshops, on-campus interviewing, experiential education opportunities (co-op program and internship programs), and others. Most of these services are available during all regular business hours. Some specialized services are available only at certain times during the week. There are many services available virtually, which you can find [here](https://career.sites.clemson.edu/virtual/ "Clemson Virtual Career and Professional Development Services").

## Academic Success Center

The Graduate Writing Consultants (GWCs) at the Writing Lab in the Academic Success Center are available for Zoom appointments and to provide feedback through email. [Here](https://www.clemson.edu/centers-institutes/writing/index.html "Academic Success Center Writing Lab") you'll be able to see available appointments times, upcoming events, and be able to make an appointment with a GWC. The Academic Success Center also offers Zoom workshops. Schedules for these workshops will be posted prior to the start of the semester.
