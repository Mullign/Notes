As you’ve seen, there are many item types: videos, readings, discussion forums, assessments. Some of these can be done individually, without the contribution of your peers. The completion of other items, including peer reviews, discussion prompts, and group work depends on the efforts of others. For these items, you have to leave enough buffer time and cannot do these items last minute.

- **Readings:** can be done alone, but make sure you prioritize required readings vs supplementary readings because required ones may be critical for you to complete the assessments.
    
- **Videos:** can be done alone. (and at your own pace: faster / slower that instructor speaks) When learning advanced concepts, you could spend more time on videos than its actual length because you may pause to think or replay certain sections.  
    
- **Quizzes:** can be done alone. (leave time to review feedback and try again if needed)
    
- **Discussion prompts:** cannot necessarily be done alone. The true value of discussion prompts come from seeing various opinions and developing critical thinking skills through comments. Make sure you plan time not only for posting the initial idea but also for viewing and replying to others.
    
- **Peer review assignments:** cannot be done alone. You need feedback from your peers to complete it, which may take a few days. Submit your assignment about 3 days before it is due. Build-in time to give your peers thoughtful feedback in time as well.
