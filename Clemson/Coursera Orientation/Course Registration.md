Visit Clemson's Student Registration Information website by [clicking here](https://www.clemson.edu/registrar/student-menu/registration/ "Student Registration Information"). There you will find information about iROAR (Clemson's registration portal), what classes Clemson is offering, and how to register for courses. [This resource](https://ccit.clemson.edu/iroaraccess/ "How to Connect to iRoar from Off-Campus") from Clemson Computing & Information Technology (CCIT) will help you access iROAR when you're not on the campus network. Make sure you have everything you'll need to register for courses by reviewing the [Registration Checklist](https://www.clemson.edu/registrar/student-menu/registration/checklist.html "Registration Checklist").

## How to Register for a Course in iROAR

You can access the iROAR Registration to browse classes, plan ahead, or complete registration by [clicking here](https://regssb.sis.clemson.edu/StudentRegistrationSsb/ssb/registration "iROAR Registration Website").

![iRoar Registration website with labels for student services offered on this website](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/DXlO7PDXTZy7WR05pggaBw_f09656dbef7f4c3293f64d998097e3f1_IROAR-Registration-page.png?expiry=1756166400000&hmac=PJgWZtYc5ly7tSdEMsLCb35xIroFWBP-sBW9O3ZcJZg)

### Browse Classes

Select a term from the dropdown menu (ex. Fall 2024). You can search for a specific course or complete a search for all courses being offered by subject (ex. CPSC - Computer Science). All offered courses will appear once you hit the "Search" button.

### Browse Course Catalog

Select a term from the dropdown menu (ex. Fall 2024). You can search for a specific course, search by subject (ex. CPSC), or simply click the Search button to see all courses and sections being offered.

### Plan Ahead

Select a term from the dropdown menu (ex. Fall 2024). Click "Create a New Plan" and search for the courses / subjects that you are interested in adding to your schedule. Once you add a course, a calendar will pop up, allowing you to add these courses to your schedule.

### View Registration Information

See your past schedules and registration information.

### Register for Classes

During your designated time, you will register for classes by clicking this tab.
