## For Students Withdrawing or Dropping Credit Hours

Students who are admitted and register for courses at Clemson University are financially responsible for all fees associated with those courses. Therefore, students are encouraged to complete all courses for which they register. If it becomes necessary for a student to drop one or more courses or completely withdraw from the University, a withdrawal or drop adjustment of academic fees may be made.

**Warning!** Withdrawing from the University may be costly. It may affect the student’s financial and academic status.

No withdrawal or drop adjustment of academic fees will be made to a semester’s tuition and fees after four weeks from the last day to register. In the case of a withdrawal from the University, adjustments will be based on the date the student drops their course(s) using the online registration system. In the case of a dropped course, adjustments will be based on the date the student drops the course using the online registration system. The student’s course drop must post prior to the beginning of the next Fall or Spring semester or subsequent summer term to be eligible for an adjustment. Adjustments for periods of four weeks or less during the Fall or Spring semester shall be made on the following basis beginning with the day following the last day to register. Students receiving Title IV Financial Aid follow a different policy.

## How to Calculate Withdrawal or Drop Adjustments

Student Financial Services must calculate withdrawal or drop adjustments based on the date of the drop/withdrawal in the system. This date is **not** determined or controlled by Student Financial Services.

- Student adjustments for tuition, activity and technology fees are calculated based on the student’s liable hours after the drop/withdrawal.
    
- Liable hours are calculated as the total hours a student is still registered plus the liable portion of the dropped/withdrawn course(s). No refunds are generated for students whose liable hours are 12 or more.
    
- The liable portion is computed by taking the number of dropped credit hours times the liability rate (100 percent **minus** the refund percentage).
    
- The adjustment amount is determined to be the difference between the original assessment and the assessment based on the new liable hours. No adjustment is due if the original assessment is less than or equal to the liable hour assessment.
