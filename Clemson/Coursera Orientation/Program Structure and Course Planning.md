This Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS) program will prepare you to navigate and shape the future of technology through a human-centric approach to computing.

You’ll acquire a broad base of foundational principles and best practices in essential areas like software development and engineering. You’ll also explore advanced topics such as:

- Data mining
    
- Complex algorithms
    
- Machine learning
    
- Artificial intelligence
    
- Database management
    
- Large-scale distributed system design and management
    

As you progress through the program, you’ll be able to focus on your own particular areas of interest - providing you with the knowledge, skills and tools to participate and thrive in the world’s most dynamic and transformational industries.

## **Program Requirements**

Learners will be required to take 10 courses (30 credits). They can apply to the program immediately or use their work in the Pathway (PBA) courses for admission. These courses are the first two courses in the MSCS program and learners will earn 3 credits from each course. Additionally, learners will need to take 8 core courses, each worth 3 credits. All courses, including the Pathway courses, must be completed within 6 years.

The Pathway courses include **CPSC 8400 Design and Analysis of Algorithms** and **CPSC 8740 AI-Receptive Software Development**. After completion of these two courses, learners who receive a B (3.0 GPA) or higher will be eligible to continue in the Master of Science in Computer Science Program. **All learners who apply for the program will need to submit an official transcript.** Learners will need to submit the official transcript during their first semester (before registration), or they will not be able to register for the next semester. For more information on transcripts, see the **Final Official Transcripts** **Procedures** in the PDF below.

### Deferred Admission (After Submitting an Official Transcript and Being Accepted into the MSCS Program)

You may defer enrollment for up to one year beyond the semester in your offer of admission, pending approval from the program of study. To defer admission, you must request and receive written approval from the academic program that recommended admission via the [GS Change of Term Form](https://media.clemson.edu/graduate/website/files/pdfs/changeoftermform.pdf "Change of Term Admission form from the Graduate School"). Your application and supporting materials are valid for one year. Should you wish to defer your admission beyond one year of the initial semester desired, a new application and supporting documentation must be submitted. If you are an international student, you should notify the Office of International Affairs when the admitting program approves your request for deferred admission.
