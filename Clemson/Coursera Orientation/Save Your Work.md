We have all experienced the frustration of hitting _Submit_ in an online form only to have all of our answers erased. Here are some tips to avoid this from happening on the Coursera platform. 

1. Watch for emails about planned Coursera outages and avoid working in the platform at those times. 

2. Check the [Learner Help Center](https://learner.coursera.help/hc "Coursera Learner Help Center") for banner notifications. Coursera will post there if there is an unexpected outage.

3. Unplanned outages can happen at any time. We also recommend the following:

- Develop and save answers outside the platform before submitting (especially for longer works like problem sets, essays, etc.).
    
- In times where you cannot work on answers outside of the platform, take a screenshot before hitting the _Submit_ button.
