## **In-video note-taking** 

On the Coursera platform, there are two ways to save notes when watching a video. 

1. Highlight parts of the transcript to save a note. You can even type in your own notes to the note you save.
    

![Screenshot. Video transcript with Notes dialog box open to the right of the transcript.](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/l7HXzd4tEemhaQ6bnADF5g_f747295a107f18f52762d8d75a524c60_Notes2.png?expiry=1756166400000&hmac=DOjTOR5MFD7NvWxfZMsUuUGJWlec6aLpXfnAjibVY7o)

Screenshot. Video transcript with text highlighted. Red arrow pointing to the right with Notes dialog box open for notes to be taken.

2. Click on the Save Note button under the video.

![Screenshot. In-page video with red arrow pointing downward to highlight Save Note button.](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/kKr7894tEemy9AqFZZTpJA_6b756938111e22251a1bd4a11cf0a63d_Notes1.png?expiry=1756166400000&hmac=ifrcb-LWqJJqJ08MyqVbGP9Xlg9JnbBHNA8lSDeE5Vk)

All your notes in the course can be found in the left sidebar under the Notes tab.

![Screenshot. Course Notes page with Course menu on left side of screen.](https://d3c33hcgiwev3.cloudfront.net/imageAssetProxy.v1/pIM--d4tEemzYQ60aJAwVA_21a46f6732677f31eb7ef7ceb2d04ee9_Notes3.png?expiry=1756166400000&hmac=PZhgXZjBfdaJq6QmkL5wTol_PmHgulCgnGRYtZ-3qbM)

Screenshot: Course Notes page with Course menu on left side of screen and Notes selected. All notes in this course shown on right side of screen.

**Note:** You cannot download or export the notes off-platform. Notes can only be referenced in the Notes tab.
