​In summary, we have studied the recent trend on private-preserving data mining, including ​privacy-preserving between users and data owners. ​And then we also studied privacy-preserving between the data owners. ​The private-preserving between the user and the data owner, we used a differential privacy ​approach. ​And then privacy-preserving between data owners, we used federated learning. ​The human-algorithm interaction focused on crowdsourcing human-in-the-loop machine-in-the-loop ​human-machine teaming. ​For beyond-accuracy in data mining applications, fairnessness, interoperability, and robustness ​were studied. ​The fairnessness includes group fairness, individual fairness, and counterfactual fairness. 

​We also studied how data mining impacts the social good and also gave examples on applications ​for social data mining applications for social good.
