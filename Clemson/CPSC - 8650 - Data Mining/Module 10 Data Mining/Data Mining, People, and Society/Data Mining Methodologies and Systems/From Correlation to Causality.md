​As we know that we have studied the coronation, coronation analysis, but how about the cosetic? ​Coronation analysis is to study the statistic association between different observed variables. ​A causality analysis is to study the causality relation between observed variables. ​So there are two kinds of causality analysis tasks. ​One is called the causality discovery. ​Another is causality inference. ​For causality discovery, we can use constraint-based algorithms, score-based algorithms, or functional causal models. 

​For causality inferences, regression adjustment, propensity score method, and covariate balancing methods can be used. ​With causality analysis, we can enhance the interoperability of black box deep learning models to help avoid unfair results and also improve the robustness of the models. ​This is a taxonomy of causality analysis. ​Causality analysis includes causality discovery, traditional methods, and big data methods. ​And also causality inference will include with observed confounders or without unobserved confounders, and also big data methods.
