​Another recent trend on data mining is to study how data mining impacts people and society ​and how people can be involved in the data mining process. ​This research includes privacy-preserving data mining and human-algorithm interaction. ​And also, in the past, most data mining tasks focused on maximizing accuracy, and recent ​research focused on fairness and interpretability and the robustness of the data mining algorithms. ​And there's a bunch of applications recently on data mining for social good.
