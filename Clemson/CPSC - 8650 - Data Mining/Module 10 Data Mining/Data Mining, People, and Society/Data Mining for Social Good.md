​For public safety and generalized linear models, naive Bayesian algorithms ​are often used. For transportation, the demand and supply model, multi-agent ​reinforcement learning were often used. There are several challenges on such applications. ​The first one is the data scarcity. In different domains, you may need different ​domain-specific evaluation metrics. And then another challenge is that you need ​a human involved in the loop.
