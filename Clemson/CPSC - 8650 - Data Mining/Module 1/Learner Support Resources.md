# There are many ways in which learners can get support from peers and/or the instructional team:

## Discussion Forums

Our program staff are here to support your learning. If you have any questions about the content covered in a course, please post it in the discussion forum. We will get back to you within 24 hours. By posting in the forum, you are also letting other students benefit from the response. To view the Discussion Forums in this course, click **Discussion Forums** on the left side of the page.

## Program Support Email Address*

If you have questions regarding your grades or assignments**, degree planning, course registration, tuition or fees, request for letter of course completion, a private issue that only affects you, **then please directly reach out to:** [**mscscoursera@clemson.edu**](mailto:mscscoursera@clemson.edu "Email Program Support")_._

*****Please note that this email is monitored by Coursera and Clemson staff to route your inquiry to the appropriate resource. Therefore, any information transmitted to or from this email address may be accessed and reviewed by other authorized personnel. If you have an issue you prefer to discuss privately, we recommend providing only general information, so your issue can be routed to the appropriate resource for further discussion.

**Do NOT send any requests directly to the university email addresses of Course/Teaching Assistants. If your question does not fall under any of the above categories, please email all other requests to [**mscscoursera@clemson.edu**](mailto:mscscoursera@clemson.edu "Email Program Support")**.**

## Slack Channel

Please log in directly to Master of Science in Computer Science Slack workspace at [https://join.slack.com/t/master-cs-clemson/signup](https://join.slack.com/t/master-cs-clemson/signup "Sign Up for a New Account with the Master of Science in Computer Science Slack") using your **@clemson.edu** email address.

Slack is a tool for you to get to know your peers and build community within the Master of Science in Computer Science program. Each degree course has a public channel in Slack that you can join to chat in real-time with all your classmates. Note that course staff will not be available via Slack.
