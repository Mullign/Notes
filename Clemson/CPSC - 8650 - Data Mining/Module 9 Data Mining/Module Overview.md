​In this module, we are going to study outliner detection. ​We'll start with the basic concept. ​Then we study different approaches for outliner detection. ​Then we'll also study different kinds of outliners. ​And then we will study how to detect the outliners in high-dimensional data. ​So what is the motivation for outliner detection? ​For example, how do we detect the credit card transactions such as transactions of unusual amount ​or multiple on-site transactions within 10 minutes or 5 minutes in two different locations where they are hundreds of miles away? 

​Now, most transactions are normal, only very few are abnormal. ​So outliner detection is also known as anomaly detection. ​It is the process of finding outliners or anomalies that the data object with behaviors that are very different from the expectation. ​There are many applications that use outliner detection, such as medical care, public safety, security, industry damage detection, ​image processing, sensor and radio network surveillance, national security, and system intrusion detection. ​Outliner detection is a kind of mining process. It is different from the clustering. ​They are two highly related but different tasks. 

​They have different purposes. ​The clustering is to find the major patterns in the data set and organize the data accordingly. ​Outliner detection tries to capture those exceptional cases that deviate substantially from the majority patterns. ​Different in their methodologies as well, outliner detection might also use supervision during the detection process, ​but the clustering analysis is typically unsupervised in nature. ​Now, even though we also studied some semi-supervised clustering analysis methods, ​clustering analysis is typically an unsupervised mining process.
