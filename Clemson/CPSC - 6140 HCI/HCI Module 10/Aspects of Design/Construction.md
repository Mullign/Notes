So the McKay-McKay toolkits ​comprise of a printed circuit board with the Arduino microcontroller, alligator clips, and USB ​cables. It communicates with the computer to send key press, mouse clicks, and mouse movements. There ​are six inputs, the four arrow keys, the space bar, and the mouse click positioned on the front of the ​board that alligator clips are clipped onto in order to connect with the computer via the USB ​cable. The other end of the clips can be attached to any non-insulating object such as a vegetable ​or a piece of fruit.
