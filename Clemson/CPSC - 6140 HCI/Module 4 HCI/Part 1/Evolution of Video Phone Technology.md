Various usability problems were identified as contributing to this reluctance to talk with video images of other people. So if they came very close to the video window, then their head was no longer captured by the camera, so it would not project them on the other side, since they were no longer under the camera and also out of the microphone range, meaning that they could not be seen or heard.
