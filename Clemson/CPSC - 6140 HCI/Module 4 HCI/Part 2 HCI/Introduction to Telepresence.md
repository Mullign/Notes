![[Introduction to Telepresence Slides.pdf]]A related technology is telepresence. Telepresence is basically a technology that is designed to allow a person to feel as if they were present in another real-world location. This includes aspects of the technology, such as projecting their body movements, actions, voice, and facial expressions, that is namely the user is transformed to the other location or a person that the user is talking to. A classic example of this is technologies like Skype, for example. Also, another type of system could be to superimpose images of the other person on a workspace. This could be in your workspace or it could be, for instance, in their workspace, essentially to superimpose the image of the other person in there. I'll show you an example in the next slide.
