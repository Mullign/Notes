The fourth and final interaction type that we are going to discuss in our course is the exploration interaction type and exploration involves users moving through the virtual or the physical environment or and exploring the environment. Physical environments with embedded sensor technologies could also allow users to explore an unfamiliar computer system through natural physical exploration in the user's physical space. So as you explore the user's real world physical space then it may also be an analog and that movement or that exploration in the physical space could be mapped to movements and exploration in the virtual space in the context of the computer-based system. So these are examples of exploration interaction type.
