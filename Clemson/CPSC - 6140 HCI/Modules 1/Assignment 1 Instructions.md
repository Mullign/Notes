# Familiarization with Research Ethics and Compliance in Human-Computer Interaction Studies

You will soon be reading and writing about case studies and experiments involving human factors in which you will find descriptions of usability studies and empirical evaluations of interactive computing systems with human subjects, and will be collecting data from participants online on your end of semester project. In order to be thoroughly familiar with the state and federal policies/guidelines and protocols associated with conducting human subjects experimentation and usability studies, you will need to complete the Clemson University online Institutional Review Board Certification program that will be Assignment #1. **This assignment will have to be completed and submitted on an individual basis - it is not a team assignment!**

## Here are the directions to the online course and certification program:

1. Go to CITI IRB Training website: [https://www.citiprogram.org/](https://www.citiprogram.org/ "Citi Program About Page")
    
2. If you are a first-time user, please register and then continue.
    
3. Once you are registered, complete the "Group 1 Investigators Conducting Social and Behavioral Science Research (SBR) at Clemson University." You should pass with at least a 90% average score on all the sub-modules.
    
    1. **VERY IMPORTANT: DO NOT try to game the system by going directly to the questions for each sub-module and attempting to receive the minimum passing score by trial and error.** Your score will be deducted if you tried to game the system by attempting each module more than three times, and you will receive a '0' on this assignment. Please carefully study each sub-module, understand all the concepts, and then attempt the quiz when you are ready!
        
4. If you have already completed the CITI IRB certification within the last two years, then please go to the final step, save and submit a copy of the **Completion Report** here on Coursera.
    
5. If it's been longer than two years since you completed the CITI IRB certification, then complete the "Group 1 Investigators conducting Social and Behavioral Science Research (SBR) at Clemson University, Refresher Course."
