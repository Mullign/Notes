# Guidelines for the Organization of the Report

## General Guidelines

- The report should be **in your own words**. If using text from the textbook, you should include it using quotation marks with proper citation in the references section.
    
- The **report abstract** should state the purpose and rationale of your report such that it helps the readers understand what they gain from reading your report.
    
- The **chapter introduction** is a general introduction to the topics of the chapter, describing what the author is trying to say or present in the chapter, and why the design guidelines presented are important.
    
- Summarize the main points of the chapter in a way that displays your understanding of the concepts in your own words.
    
- All the **examples** in your report should be **computer science (CS) and/or information systems (IS) related** examples (i.e. websites, apps, computer interfaces, information dashboards in cars etc.) that you may have encountered with respect to the concept that you are asked to explain. You are **NOT** allowed to use any of the examples in the TDET, weekly discussion topic readings, or any other examples from class materials. Doing so will result in deduction of points. However, you are allowed to use examples from other resources from your own research on this topic, as long as you properly cite them in the paper.
    
- Please **do not use the same CS/IS example for all the concepts, instead use 1 unique CS/IS example per concept**. Only one unique CS/IS example should be discussed with regard to each usability concept, i.e. discuss Example 1 for Affordance, Example 2 for Signifiers, and so on. **For each concept, explain in detail accompanied by screenshots with annotations and a** **well-justified** **description of how the features of the example conforms to or supports the design guideline, or violates or poorly conforms to the design guideline.** You do not need to discuss both positive and negative aspects for the same concept with examples. **Be thorough in your descriptions clearly detailing the connection between features of the application examples and the key ideas of each of the design guidelines (the why?).** In other words, clearly justify why the feature in the application example is a good or a bad example of each of these design principles, elaborating on the connections between aspects of that feature and the key concepts and ideas of that design principle.
    
- **Conclusion** section should answer the following questions:
    
    - What makes this chapter interesting and important?
        
    - How has the reading helped you or changed the way you see the world around you?
        
    - How has the design guidelines discussed in the chapter helped you understand what is important and how to emphasize it in the design of computer applications?
        
- It is recommended to use images/pictures to support your points. However, the images need to be numbered properly, have appropriate captions, and referenced in the text. Citation needs to be provided for any image obtained from an external source.
    
- **Points will be deducted for any typographical or grammatical errors.** Please be sure to proofread your report prior to submission.
