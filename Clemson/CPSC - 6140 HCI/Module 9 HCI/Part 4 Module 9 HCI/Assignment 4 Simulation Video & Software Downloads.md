## nstructions to Download the Software

The desktop VR educational application is a beta version of a training module developed by Clemson University’s Center for Workforce Development (CUCWD) in collaboration with MATE II (Marine Advanced Technological Education Inspiration for Innovation) and NCAT (National Center for Autonomous Technologies) to create a virtual underwater ROV (Remotely Operated Vehicle) simulation where students are tasked with hunting for a type of sea star that is decimating coral reefs. MATE hosts a world-wide competition for teams from upper elementary to college age where the goal is to build an ROV and complete a set of tasks. Since the competition was cancelled due to covid, CUCWD wanted to give teams an opportunity to pilot an ROV and still compete with others, so they have developed a simple game where they hunt for sea stars to earn points. CUCWD is looking for (a) an HCI inspired critique and evaluation of their ROV simulation, and (b) redesign of the simulation for a mobile smart phone version of the simulation that they are considering developing next.

Watch the short instruction video below that provides detailed background regarding the ROV simulation goals and user experience. Then, please download and install the simulation from the first link. There are separate simulations for a Mac or a Windows PC available.

The software begins with a brief instruction before the ROV simulation game begins. You get points for neutralizing the star fish, and there is a leaderboard functionality (similar to other games). CUCWD designed this desktop VR system to be platform independent and was designed to be intuitive in learning and easy to use for users of diverse backgrounds. The simulation is designed to take input from either a keyboard (using W, A, S, D, Q, and E keys), mouse, or Gamepad (similar to a Sony Play Station Game Controller). However, as the first release version of the system, they need your expertise and assistance in redesigning the interactions for a tablet or smart phone version of the system.

Once you have watched the video, please explore the VR simulation. Make sure you are thoroughly familiar with the tasks and are comfortable with the system. Familiarize yourself with all aspects of the system before you continue with the assignment.
