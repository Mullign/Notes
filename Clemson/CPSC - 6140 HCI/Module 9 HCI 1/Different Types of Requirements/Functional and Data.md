​Now, if a system is under construction for trading of commodities and stocks, ​then data must be up-to-date and accurate in such a system and is likely to ​change many times a day based on ​the latest real-time data that the system gathers. ​So you may think about the accuracy and validity and ​the real-time nature of the data of such a system as a functional requirement. ​In personal banking domain, ​data must be accurate, ​must persist over many months and probably years, ​and there is likely to be a lot of it. ​So that might be the functional requirements here. ​So the functional requirements have to do with what the system should do, ​and the non-functional requirements might be additional aspects such as security, ​response time, for example, ​and the need for the application to work on different platforms. ​With regards to data, ​you can think about what kinds of data need to be ​stored and how will they be stored? ​Will there be a back-end database?
