​The environment or a context of use is also a very important requirement. ​For example, if you're thinking about an ATM machine, then if the product is an ATM machine, ​then potentially it needs to be placed where it might be dusty, noisy, there could potentially ​be lots of vibrations, there might be differences in light, heat, and humidity. ​So you want to take into consideration the diverse circumstances, environmental circumstances, ​in which the hardware of the computational system might actually reside. ​The social aspects have to do with sharing of files, of displays. ​So maybe there's privacy concerns with regard to if displays are positioned in public settings ​such that one could potentially look over the shoulder or look from a different vantage ​point of what someone else is working on. ​And so maybe you need to consider making it such that only the content on the display ​shows up in a specific vantage point. ​Other social aspects could be across great distances, communication, it needs to operate ​in a synchronous manner with other users' operations on the system, and privacy for ​clients could be one of the issues.
