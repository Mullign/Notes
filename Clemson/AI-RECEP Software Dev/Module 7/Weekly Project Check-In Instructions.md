## Objective:

Provide weekly updates on your progress with the Personalized Movie Recommendation System project. These updates should be posted in the designated discussion forum and should detail your accomplishments, challenges, and plans for the upcoming week.

## Weekly Project Check-In Structure:

1. **Summary of Accomplishments:**
    

- **What did you complete?** Describe the tasks and milestones you accomplished during the week. Be specific about the features you developed, data you processed, models you trained, or interfaces you designed.
    
- **How did you achieve this?** Explain the methods and tools you used to complete these tasks. Mention any AI tools, libraries, or frameworks that were particularly helpful.
    

1. **Challenges and Issues:**
    

- **What obstacles did you encounter?** Discuss any difficulties or roadblocks you faced during the week. This could include technical issues, data problems, or conceptual challenges.
    
- **How did you address them?** Explain the steps you took to overcome these challenges. If the issue remains unresolved, describe your plans for addressing it in the future.
    

1. **Plans for Next Week:**
    

- **What are your goals?** Outline the tasks and objectives you plan to focus on in the coming week. Be specific about the features you aim to develop, data you plan to collect or preprocess, or models you intend to train or refine.
    
- **What resources will you need?** Identify any tools, data, or support you will need to accomplish your goals. Mention if you need feedback or assistance from peers or instructors.
    

1. **Reflection and Learnings:**
    

- **What did you learn?** Reflect on any new insights or skills you gained during the week. This could include technical knowledge, project management techniques, or lessons learned from overcoming challenges.
    
- **How will this impact your project?** Discuss how your learnings will influence your approach to the project moving forward. Mention any adjustments or improvements you plan to make based on your experiences.

## Submission Instructions:

- Post your weekly update in the designated discussion forum by the due date each week.
    
- Engage with **at least two** classmates' updates by providing constructive feedback, asking questions, or offering suggestions.
    

These updates are crucial for tracking your progress and ensuring that you stay on track with your project goals. They also provide an opportunity to reflect on your learning journey and receive valuable feedback from peers and instructors.
