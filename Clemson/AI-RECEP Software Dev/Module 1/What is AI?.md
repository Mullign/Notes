So ==**Artificial intelligence, more commonly known as AI,==** 

**==is the field of study and development of computer systems capable of==** 

**==performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.==** 

**==These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem solving, perception,==** 

**==and language understanding.**== 

AI integrates disciplines like computer science and psychology, and math and 

neuroscience to create systems that can adapt, improve from experiences, and 

operate on its own.
