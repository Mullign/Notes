### Module Learning Objectives:

**Learners will be able to...**

1. define and explain the core concepts of human-centered computing, emphasizing people-first design and understanding user contexts **(CLO 1, CLO 3)**.
2. identify and analyze human factors in computing systems, such as usability, accessibility, and the impact of social science methodologies like participatory design and ethnography **(CLO 2, CLO 3, CLO 4)**.
3. articulate the importance of human-centered design in AI – understanding societal impacts, addressing biases, and promoting fair practices **(CLO 2, CLO 4, CLO 7)**.
