The integrative project is individual. The main goal of the project is to apply the HCC, AI, and HCAI methodologies for the evaluation of Human-Centered AI solutions.

The project does not require coding skills. It can use publicly deployed AI models such as ChatGPT, the OpenAI API, or any other tool that can promote prototyping. There is no constraint on the AI technology that you want to use.

**Project Types**

The project can have one of the following outcomes:

- **A research paper (or technical report) to be published at a conference or on ArXiv.**
    
    - The research paper should have regular sections of an HCI/AI paper, such as Abstract, Introduction, Related Work, Solution Description, Methodology, Evaluation/Results, Discussion, and Conclusion.
        
    - The paper (or technical report) can be structured as regular publication formats: Work-In-Progress (~6 pages), Poster (with a 3 pages report), Demo (Code + 3 pages report), Pictorials (with a 3 pages report), short paper (~8 pages), or full paper (~16 pages).
        
    - It is not required to be published but try to do your best. Published content gets full credit without following the course criteria.
        
    - Blog posts are allowed but should provide a link to the paper or technical report.
        
- **Entrepreneurial Project**
    
    - Document all the product designs and decisions based on the user.
        
    - The project has to have a demo to test.
        
    - In case you have code, it should be shared, at least privately.
        
    - The project must be published in public listings such as ProductHunt, Futurepedia, etc.
        
- **Both**
    
    - A public working demo plus its report are considered for this category.
        
    - A full grade is granted to these projects if they are successfully implemented.

**Checkpoints**

The project idea can change or evolve over time. You can use your current research work as long as you justify HCAI methodologies in it. Novel ideas are encouraged. You can use ChatGPT or other tools for the ideation stage of the project but not for writing it (only for grammar and formatting).

#### **The project will be evaluated in three checkpoints:**

- **Checkpoint 1: Project Definition (Module 4)** must include:
    
    - Project title
        
    - Project summary
        
    - Methods used from
        
        - HCC
            
        - AI
            
        - HCAI
            
        - Survey / Interview Script / Participatory Design
            
- **Checkpoint 2: Project Execution (Module 9)** must include:
    
    - Collected data analysis
        
    - Design decisions
        
    - Prototype proposal
        
    - Evaluation methods
        
    - Technicalities of the project
        
- **Checkpoint 3: Project System Evolution (Module 14)** must include:
    
    - All previous content
        
    - Related Work
        
    - Evaluation results
        
    - Insights
        
    - Takeaways
