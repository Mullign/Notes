### Module Learning Objectives:

**Learners will be able to...**

1. define key terms related to human-AI collaboration and contrast them with automation **(CLO 1)**.
2. trace major historical milestones that shaped current human-AI collaboration capabilities **(CLO 1)**.
3. summarize major empirical findings on human-AI synergy, augmentation, and design challenges **(CLO 1)**.
4. identify how journals, news reflections, and projects integrate with course themes **(CLO 1)**.
5. describe the evolution of human-AI collaboration, referencing key historical and modern milestones **(CLO 1)**.
6. differentiate roles that AI can take (tool, assistant, teammate) and explain when each is appropriate **(CLO 1, CLO 2)**.
7. explain the differences between automation, autonomy, and collaboration **(CLO 1, CLO 2)**.
8. identify major behavioral, cognitive, and physiological factors that influence effective collaboration **(CLO 1, CLO 2)**.
